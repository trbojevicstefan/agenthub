# Opaya: "One place. All your agents." (3D film): voiceover script

Every part starts on an even second, so each part change lands on a bar line of the music (120 BPM). Start each voiceover line at its part's timestamp. Each line fits its part at a natural pace (about 2.3 words per second), with room to breathe.

## Notes
- Everything is animated in 3D (three.js): one continuous camera move through one scene, with no slides and no screen recordings. It uses the Opaya brand: the logo as a 3D emblem, mint #4FE39A and emerald #00A675, and DM Sans with IBM Plex Mono. Agent logos are the official ones Opaya itself uses.
- The storyline and wording follow our earlier Opaya marketing video. On-screen text is large, on a darkened left side, and readable without sound.
- The music is original, synthesized (no samples), in a bright major key. It drops out when the agent breaks (1:10) and comes back when the Opaya Agent fixes it (1:18). It peaks around -5 dBFS. Under a voiceover, lower the music by 8–10 dB or duck it; the effects can stay as they are.
- As in the earlier video, "Opaya Vault" and "up to 20x faster" are marketing wording. The app itself does not use the name "Vault" (keys are stored with OS encryption and shared by name), and Opaya does not measure the 20x figure. Check you're comfortable with both before publishing. The numbers on screen (6 agents, 0:12, flight prices) are illustrative examples.
